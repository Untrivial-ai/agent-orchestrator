## Short answer

Choose **Agent Orchestrator (AO)** when you need a project orchestrator and an automatic PR/CI/review loop under Apache-2.0. Choose **Paseo** when you want a solo-maintained multi-surface client (desktop, iOS, Android, web, CLI, TypeScript SDK) to drive agents on your machines and you do not need an orchestrator agent or a built-in PR/CI feedback loop.

Paseo's alternatives pages omit AO; this page exists so the comparison is explicit and current. [Paseo](https://paseo.sh/) is a self-hosted interface for several coding agents. [AO](https://orchestrator.inc/docs/) is a delivery-oriented orchestrator around those agents.

## What AO uniquely has

- A **project orchestrator** that plans, spawns, and redirects workers.
- **CI-failure and review-comment routing** tied to the owning session and branch.
- **GitHub and GitLab** delivery facts in the same board as the agent (Linear still in progress).
- **Apache-2.0** license and ~25 harnesses with an isolated browser per worker.

## Where Paseo is stronger

- **Client surface breadth**: desktop, iOS, Android, web, CLI, and a TypeScript SDK, including an optional end-to-end encrypted relay for remote pairing.
- **Voice control** and a privacy posture that advertises no telemetry or forced logins.
- A lighter product if you only want one UI over Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, OpenCode, and Pi—without AO's orchestrator or PR loop opinions.
- Free with a **custom license** (not Apache); verify terms for your use case.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are AO and Paseo both multi-device?

Yes, with different trust models. AO's phone apps pair to your desktop daemon over an opt-in LAN listener (home-network plaintext by design). Paseo also ships mobile/web/CLI clients and can pair through a relay when you enable it.

### Why isn't AO on Paseo's alternatives list?

Product directories drift. This page is AO's current, verifiable comparison so buyers are not left comparing against stale third-party copy.

### When is Paseo the better fit?

When you want the widest client matrix and SDK today, and you will own orchestration/PR process yourself. Pick AO when the orchestrator and CI/review routing are the reason you are shopping.
