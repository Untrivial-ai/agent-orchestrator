## Pair your phone over a protected local connection <PRBadge url="https://github.com/Untrivial-ai/agent-orchestrator/pull/2533" />

Connect Mobile opens a temporary password-protected local connection and pairs a phone by QR code without exposing the loopback daemon.

## Point to changes directly in the browser preview <PRBadge url="https://github.com/Untrivial-ai/agent-orchestrator/pull/2548" />

You can mark a point in an in-app browser preview and send that visual feedback back to the agent.

## Switch agents without losing session work <PRBadge url="https://github.com/Untrivial-ai/agent-orchestrator/pull/2412" />

A running session can change agents while keeping its project, branch, worktree, and uncommitted changes.
