## A new foundation for AO

From late May through June 24, most development moved to [a focused rewrite workspace](https://github.com/aoagents/ReverbCode). AO was rebuilt around a Go daemon and Electron desktop app, with durable local state and clearer boundaries between the interface, services, runtimes, and integrations. The work returned here on June 24 and became the foundation for the releases that followed.
